# List of Breached Japanese Organisations

## Times Car

Company Profile: car-sharing service

Breach detected date: 2026-09-25

Breach reported date: 2026-09-25

Impact: up to 6.6 million accounts's data is stolen 

(possibly) Leaked data:

- name
- address
- date of birth
- telephone number
- email address
- membership number
- driver's license information
- identity verification documents
- corporate membership information
- linked service ID

(Confirmed) Leaked data:

- Driver's license images
- Current address verification document images (utility bills, etc.)
- Student ID images (for student plans)
- Family verification document images (for family plans)

Source: "[タイムズカーWebシステムへの不正アクセスに関する調査結果および今後の対応について（第3報）](https://www.park24.co.jp/news/2026/09/20260929-1.html)" announcement by the company.
